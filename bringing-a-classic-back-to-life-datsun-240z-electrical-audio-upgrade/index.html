Bringing a Classic Back to Life: Datsun 240Z Electrical & Audio Upgrade

May 7, 2026 · Albert's Auto Electrical, Smithfield NSW

There’s something special about working on iconic classics — and this Datsun 240Z was no exception. When it came into the workshop, the goal was clear: improve reliability, modernise key features, and preserve the timeless character that makes the 240Z such a legend on the road.

🔧🚗✨ At Albert’s Auto Electrical, we carried out a full range of electrical repairs, lighting upgrades, and audio improvements to bring this classic back to its best. Electrical Repairs & Diagnostic Work Like many vintage vehicles, this Datsun had a few electrical issues that needed attention.

Our team carried out detailed diagnostic work to identify wiring faults and problem areas throughout the vehicle. We repaired damaged wiring, secured loose connections, and cleaned up untidy wiring to improve both reliability and overall presentation.

A clean electrical system not only improves performance but also helps prevent future issues — especially in older classics where wiring can become worn over time. Modern Lighting Upgrade with JW Speaker LEDs To improve visibility and safety while maintaining the car’s classic appearance, we upgraded the headlights with a premium JW Speaker LED H4 setup.

The result is a brighter, clearer beam pattern for better night driving and a modern lighting performance that suits the vehicle perfectly. We also installed new LED parker globes, giving the front end a cleaner and sharper look while improving efficiency and reliability.

Clean Audio Upgrade with a Classic Feel No restoration or upgrade is complete without great sound. To modernise the audio system without taking away from the car’s vintage charm, we installed Pioneer 6-inch speakers along with a custom radio bracket and rear speaker setup.

The finished result delivers crisp, quality audio while keeping the interior looking neat, functional, and true to the style of the original car. A Pleasure Working on an Icon Projects like this are always rewarding. The Datsun 240Z remains one of the most iconic Japanese sports cars ever built, and it’s always a privilege to help keep these classics on the road with modern electrical solutions and upgrades.

A big thank you to the owner for trusting Albert’s Auto Electrical with this beauty. 📍 Need electrical repairs, lighting upgrades, diagnostics, or custom installations for your vehicle?
