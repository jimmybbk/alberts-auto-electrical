Our Story

Quality work and honest advice: that's the Albert's way

At Albert's Auto Electrical, we deliver specialist auto electrical services to a diverse range of vehicles. With 25 years of experience behind him, Albert Costagliola and his small team are known across Sydney for fault-finding that actually finds the fault, wiring that's routed like a factory harness, and advice you can take to the bank.

From weekend race cars and cherished classics to the family 4WD and light commercial trucks, we treat every vehicle like it's our own, because our name is on the door and our reputation rides out with every car we fix.

What We Stand For

The values behind every job

Five principles have carried us from day one. They shape how we quote, how we work and how we treat your car.

Quality

High-quality workmanship on every vehicle: neat looms, correct connectors and repairs that last, not band-aids.

Trust

Customers hand us their prized classics and race cars and come back year after year. That trust is earned job by job.

Honesty

If it's a $12 fix under warranty, we'll tell you. If a part can be replaced free under warranty, we'll send you there. No upselling, ever.

Performance

From ECU tuning support to drag-strip wiring, we build electrical systems that deliver and hold together when it matters.

Reliability

Quotes on time, work on schedule, phones answered. When we say your car will be ready Thursday, it's ready Thursday.

Ready when you are

Book your car in for a diagnosis or a full rewire and we'll take it from there.

The Team

Meet Albert Costagliola

Managing Director Albert Costagliola has been working on cars since he was 15. Cars have always been a passion of his, and he has since made it his career. Delivering better performance and neatening up wiring are some of the things he loves most about his job.

Bringing this passion to Albert's Auto Electrical, Albert founded his business in 2017. With 25 years of experience and a love for what he does, you can rely on his expert advice and specialist skills to deliver the performance and electrical solutions you're after.

  • Working on cars since he was 15
  • 25 years of auto electrical experience
  • Founded Albert's Auto Electrical in 2017
The Team

Meet Jayde

Jayde joined us in August 2023 as our newest Tradesman Auto Electrician, and he's been part of the family ever since. He brought with him a wealth of experience in the automotive electrical industry and, like Albert, a real passion for cars.

On the tools he's a great team player who's always willing to go the extra mile to get the job done. Away from the workshop he enjoys time with family and friends, trips to the beach and sport, and being a keen learner he's always looking for new ways to take his skills further.
