Bringing Classic Ford Luxury into the Modern Era ⚡️

Jan 26, 2026 · Albert's Auto Electrical, Smithfield NSW

There’s something timeless about a classic Ford Fairlane 500. The long lines, the refined presence, the unmistakable character—it’s pure automotive heritage. But behind every great classic build is a question: how do you preserve that legacy while making it reliable and enjoyable on modern roads?

That’s exactly what this Fairlane set out to achieve when it rolled into Albert’s Auto Electrical . A Modern Foundation, Built to Last This project began where it matters most—behind the dash. We carried out a complete front-to-back rewire , creating a clean, reliable electrical system designed for safety, longevity, and future upgrades.

No shortcuts. Just solid workmanship and precision from the ground up. Digital Innovation, Period-Correct Feel At the heart of the transformation is a Haltech IC-10 digital display , seamlessly integrated with a reverse camera for modern driving confidence.

To keep the interior true to its roots, the system was housed in a custom-designed screen that preserves the original dash aesthetic. From the driver’s seat, it still feels unmistakably classic. Under the surface, it’s a different story—modern performance, accuracy, and control at your fingertips.

Respecting Heritage, Embracing the Future This build is a perfect example of what happens when cutting-edge technology meets classic Ford styling . Every decision was guided by attention to detail and respect for the vehicle’s history—enhancing functionality without compromising character.

The result? A Fairlane that looks right, feels right, and is built right. Classic looks. Modern control. Built right. 🔧🚘
