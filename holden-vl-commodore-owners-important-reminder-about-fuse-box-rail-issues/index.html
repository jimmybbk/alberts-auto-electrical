Holden VL Commodore Owners — Important Reminder About Fuse Box Rail Issues!

Dec 8, 2025 · Albert's Auto Electrical, Smithfield NSW

If you’re the proud owner of a Holden VL Commodore, you already know how special these classics are. But as these vehicles age, certain components begin to show their weaknesses — and one of the most common (and overlooked) issues we’re seeing more frequently is a melted fuse box rail .

Recently, another VL rolled into our workshop with the exact same problem. While it might seem minor at first glance, a damaged or melted fuse box rail can quickly snowball into major electrical faults, unreliable performance, and even pose safety risks if ignored.

⚠️ Why Does the Fuse Box Rail Melt? Over time, heat buildup, ageing wiring, and increased electrical load can cause the rail to melt or deform. Many VLs still run their factory fuse boxes , which were never designed to handle modern electrical accessories or decades of wear.

A failing fuse box rail can lead to: Intermittent electrical issues Blown fuses Poor connections Risk of short circuits Potential fire hazards This is one of those problems that gets worse the longer it’s left unchecked. 🔧 How We Can Help At Albert’s Auto Electrical in Sydney , we specialise in diagnosing and repairing electrical faults in classic Holdens—especially the iconic VL Commodore.

Whether your fuse box rail is beginning to warp or has already melted, we can inspect, repair, or replace it to ensure your VL remains safe and reliable on the road. 📅 Don’t Wait for a Breakdown — Book Your Inspection If you own a VL, consider this your sign to get your fuse box rail checked before it becomes a bigger issue.
