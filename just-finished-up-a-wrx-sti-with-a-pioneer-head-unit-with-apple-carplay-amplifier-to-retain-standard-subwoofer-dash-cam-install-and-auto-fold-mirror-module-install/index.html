admin_albertsautoelectrical September 4, 2023 September 29, 2023 0 comments Just finished up a WRX STI with a Pioneer head unit with Apple CarPlay, amplifier to retain standard subwoofer, dash cam install, and auto fold mirror module install.

The Pioneer head unit is a great upgrade, it adds Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, as well as a number of other features. The amplifier is a great way to improve the sound quality, and it allows you to retain the standard subwoofer.

The dash cam is a great way to record your driving, and it can be a lifesaver if you ever get into an accident. The auto fold mirror module is a convenient way to fold your mirrors when you park, it can also help to prevent theft. I’m really happy with the way this build turned out, it’s a great way to improve the functionality and performance of your WRX STI.
