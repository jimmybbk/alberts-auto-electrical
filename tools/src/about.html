Our Story

Quality work, honest advice — that's the Albert's way

At Albert's Auto Electrical, we deliver specialist auto electrical services to a diverse range of vehicles. With over 20 years of experience, Albert Costagliola and his team are known across Sydney for fault-finding that actually finds the fault, wiring that's routed like a factory harness, and advice you can take to the bank.

From weekend race cars and cherished classics to the family 4WD and light commercial trucks, we treat every vehicle like it's our own — because our name is on the door and our reputation rides out with every car we fix.

What We Stand For

The values behind every job

Five principles have carried us from day one — they shape how we quote, how we work and how we treat your car.

Quality

High-quality workmanship on every vehicle — neat looms, correct connectors and repairs that last, not band-aids.

Trust

Customers hand us their prized classics and race cars — and come back year after year. That trust is earned job by job.

Honesty

If it's a $12 fix under warranty, we'll tell you. If a part can be replaced free under warranty, we'll send you there. No upselling, ever.

Performance

From ECU tuning support to drag-strip wiring, we build electrical systems that deliver — and hold together when it matters.

Reliability

Quotes on time, work on schedule, phones answered. When we say your car will be ready Thursday, it's ready Thursday.
