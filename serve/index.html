Service 01

Performance Cars

Offering high-quality auto electrical services for performance cars, from street builds to full drag and circuit machines. We supply, fit and wire engine management systems, and build wiring that survives heat, vibration and 7,000 rpm.

  • ECU supply, fitting & wiring, plug-in and wire-in
  • Engine conversions & half-cut rewiring
  • Custom and piggyback ECUs & engine management upgrades
  • Performance wiring, body & engine harnesses
  • Gauges, datalogging & engine protection setups
Engine management brands we work with
Race-proven wiring
Service 02

Classic Cars

Need a reliable auto electrician for your classic? We are fully qualified to provide auto electrical services. We understand that a classic car is not just about the journey - it is a story. Every single rewire is planned, labelled, and routed the way the factory wished it had.

  • Complete full rewires: body, engine & lighting circuits
  • Repair & restoration of tired or butchered wiring
  • Custom wiring harnesses built in-house
  • Gauge, instrument & dashboard rewiring
  • Period-correct finishes with modern reliability
Factory-neat looms
Service 03

Cars, Trucks & Four-Wheel Drives

Delivering reliable auto electrical services that solve everyday problems, plus proper 4WD and truck accessories fitted once, fitted right. If it clicks, cranks, glows or glows too dim, we'll find out why.

  • Batteries, alternators & starter motors
  • Diagnostics & engine light faults
  • Dual battery systems & DC-DC chargers
  • Driving lights, spotties, light bars & truck lighting
  • Electric brake controllers & trailer plug wiring
  • UHF / CB radios, reversing cameras & headlights/taillights
Tour-ready 4WD fit-outs
Service 04

Installation of A/C Units & Air-Con Service

Summers are brutal in Sydney! We can install, service, and even fix air conditioning not only for cars, but also for 4WDs and trucks, and retrofitting to cars which did not have A/C to ensure you stay icy-cold.

  • A/C system installation & retrofits
  • Air-con regas, leak testing & diagnosis
  • Compressor, condenser & blower repairs
  • Cabin air & heating faults sorted
Ice-cold all summer
