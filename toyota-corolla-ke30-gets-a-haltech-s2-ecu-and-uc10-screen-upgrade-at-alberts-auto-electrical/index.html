Toyota Corolla KE30 Gets a Haltech S2 ECU and UC10 Screen Upgrade at Albert’s Auto Electrical

Apr 3, 2025 · Albert's Auto Electrical, Smithfield NSW

Discover how Albert’s Auto Electrical transformed a classic Toyota Corolla KE30 with a Haltech S2 ECU, custom engine harness, and a UC10 digital dash for modern performance and control. Bringing the KE30 Into the Modern Era At Albert’s Auto Electrical, we specialize in blending timeless style with cutting-edge technology.

Our latest project involved a clean, classic Toyota Corolla KE30, a beloved platform in the JDM and restomod scene. But while the exterior stayed true to its heritage, under the hood, it was time for a serious upgrade. The Build: Haltech S2 ECU + Custom Engine Harness The heart of this upgrade started with a Haltech Elite S2 ECU installation.

Known for its advanced tuning capabilities and reliability, the S2 brings modern engine management to older platforms, opening the door for precision control, tunability, and performance diagnostics. To ensure a clean and reliable integration, we fabricated a custom engine harness tailored specifically for the KE30’s setup.

Every connection was planned and built for durability, efficiency, and ease of future service. No universal shortcuts, just proper craftsmanship. Display Upgrade: Haltech UC10 Digital Dash To complement the new ECU, we installed the Haltech UC10 digital display, a sleek, full-color touchscreen that delivers real-time data in a format that’s easy to read and looks right at home in a modern cockpit.

With customizable layouts and sharp visual feedback, the UC10 adds both style and serious functionality to the KE30’s dashboard. Why This Matters Classic cars often lack the tech to keep up with modern tuning and diagnostics. By upgrading with a standalone ECU and digital dash, you gain: Real-time data and alerts Customizable performance tuning Modern reliability Improved drivability and engine protection For owners who want their vintage rides to perform like today’s high-tech builds, this is the perfect path forward.

Thinking of Upgrading Your Ride? Whether you’re running a classic like the KE30 or a late-model performance car, Albert’s Auto Electrical offers custom ECU installs, wiring solutions, and digital dash integrations tailored to your build.

Contact us today to talk about your next upgrade, and bring your car’s potential to life.
